Terms & Conditions

National Honey Mission • KVIC MSME

1. Authorized Apiary Telemetry: KVIC Bee Mitra application services and IoT sensors are intended for verified beekeepers participating in the National Honey Mission.

2. Proper Device Upkeep: Beekeepers are advised to keep sensor probes free of excessive propolis accumulation and replace sensor coin cells promptly.

3. Advisory Nature: Telemetry health indices, disease probability ratings, and harvest weight forecasts are algorithmic estimates based on sensor models.

4. Honey Procurement & Subsidy: Certified produce adhering to KVIC moisture benchmarks (<20%) is eligible for state procurement support.

Privacy Policy

Farmer Data Protection & Security

1. Beekeeper Data Security: Personal contact numbers, apiary locations, and sensor telemetry readings are securely encrypted under Indian digital privacy guidelines.

2. Zero Commercial Sharing: Farmer data is never rented, monetized, or shared with unauthorized third-party commercial organizations.

3. Telemetry Storage: Hourly sensor telemetry (temperature, moisture, sound) is utilized strictly to provide proactive colony alerts and disease prevention guidance.

4. Data Control: You have full rights to request telemetry archive export or deletion by contacting your designated district KVIC extension center.
